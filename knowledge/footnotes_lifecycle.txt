│     Wraps bare inline digits that were missed    │

│                                                  │
│  New fields on areas:  consolidated:true         │
│  New fields on pages:  footnote_consolidated     │

FOOTNOTE AREA — FIELD EVOLUTION SUMMARY
────────────────────────────────────────
  After step 2:      id, type, polygon, text
  After step 6:      + text cleaned, <sup> in body
  After consolidate: + consolidated:true (on merged areas)
                     + page.footnote_consolidated:true
                     + book.footnote_regime
                     + split IDs: area_003_s1, _s2 ...
  After step 7:      → HTML div.footnote-item
  After link step:   → id="fn-N", href links


CONSOLIDATION DECISION LOGIC
──────────────────────────────
  footnote area text starts with "1." and "1"
  appears in body text of this page?
  → PRIMARY (new entry)

  footnote area text has no leading number,
  OR its number is not in this page's body text?
  → CONTINUATION → append to previous area.text
                  → mark consolidated:true

  is_running_continuation:true in JSON?
  → always treat as continuation (manual override)

  is_running_continuation:false in JSON?
  → always treat as primary (manual override)
